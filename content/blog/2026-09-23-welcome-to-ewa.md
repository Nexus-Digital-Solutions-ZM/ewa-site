We're excited to launch our new home online. Elevate Women in Agroecology (EWA) exists to put women at the centre of sustainable food systems — as farmers, educators, entrepreneurs, and stewards of the land.

Through this site you'll be able to follow our GrowHer Urban Initiative, keep an eye on the upcoming Little Agroecology Explorers programme for children, and reach out if you'd like to partner with us.

More updates coming soon — thank you for growing with us.

intro_heading: What it is
intro_text: "GrowHer Urban Initiative teaches women to grow fresh vegetables in
  sack gardens — simple, low-cost planting sacks that turn a small yard, a
  rooftop corner, or a strip of communal land into a productive garden. It's
  agroecology adapted for the city: no need for acres of land, just soil, sacks,
  seed, and a bit of guidance."
intro_text_2: Women in the programme learn organic growing methods, water-wise
  techniques, and how to keep a small garden productive year-round — skills that
  feed families first, and can grow into a small income as harvests increase.
intro_image: /images/growher-2.jpg
who_heading: Who it's for
who_items:
  - label: Women in urban and peri-urban households
    desc: with little or no farmland but access to a small outdoor space.
  - label: Community groups and cooperatives
    desc: looking to grow food together and share the harvest or proceeds.
  - label: Anyone new to growing
    desc: no farming background required — training starts from the basics.
involve_heading: How to get involved
involve_text: Reach out through WhatsApp or the contact page to ask about
  joining a GrowHer group in your area, hosting a sack-gardening training, or
  supporting the initiative with materials or funding.

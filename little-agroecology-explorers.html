Little Agroecology Explorers

Animated stories of learning & adventure that shape how children see agriculture, agribusiness, and environmental protection.

What's coming

Little Agroecology Explorers is EWA's programme for children — animated learning-stories that introduce young minds to agriculture, agribusiness, and environmental stewardship in a way that's warm, playful, and easy to understand.

The first volume, Little Agroecology Explorers: Stories & Activity Book, introduces the principles of agroecology through story and activity pages designed for young children and their teachers.

  • Agriculture
    Introducing children to agroecology.
  • Agribusiness
    Farming as a viable business.
  • Environmental Protection
    Learning to be good stewards of the environment.

Why it matters

Across our communities, women grow the food that feeds families — yet too often without the training, resources, or recognition that agriculture deserves. EWA exists to change that: elevating women as agroecology leaders, protecting the land they depend on, and building food systems that last.

From urban sack gardens to school learning programmes for children, EWA meets people where they are and grows with them.

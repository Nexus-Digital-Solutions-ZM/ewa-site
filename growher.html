GrowHer Urban Initiative

Urban farming through sack gardening — growing food where space is tight and need is real.

What it is

GrowHer Urban Initiative teaches women to grow fresh vegetables in sack gardens — simple, low-cost planting sacks that turn a small yard, a rooftop corner, or a strip of communal land into a productive garden. It's agroecology adapted for the city: no need for acres of land, just soil, sacks, seed, and a bit of guidance.

Women in the programme learn organic growing methods, water-wise techniques, and how to keep a small garden productive year-round — skills that feed families first, and can grow into a small income as harvests increase.

Who it's for

  • Women in urban and peri-urban households
    with little or no farmland but access to a small outdoor space.
  • Community groups and cooperatives
    looking to grow food together and share the harvest or proceeds.
  • Anyone new to growing
    no farming background required — training starts from the basics.

How to get involved

Reach out through WhatsApp or the contact page to ask about joining a GrowHer group in your area, hosting a sack-gardening training, or supporting the initiative with materials or funding.

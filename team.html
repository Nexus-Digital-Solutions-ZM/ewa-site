Grace Kacemba-Simbule, FAIZ

Founder & CEO, Elevate Women in Agroecology (EWA)

Grace Kacemba-Simbule is a licensed agriculturalist with AIZ, a Natural Resources Manager by academia and profession and an educator having served as lecturer in both public and private higher education institutions. She is also an agroecology advocate and certified entrepreneurship mentor passionate about women's empowerment for poverty eradication, sustainable agriculture, environmental stewardship, resilient food systems and nurturing the next generation in the same through educating children on agriculture not as an option but essential survival skill.

With a decade of experience as a managerial professional in the marketplace through credible experience, she is committed to creating practical opportunities that connect agroecology with education, agripreneurship, food security and economic empowerment.

Through EWA and initiatives such as Little Agroecology Explorers (LAE), Grace is building a movement that inspires women, communities and the next generation to become active participants in creating a more sustainable and prosperous food future.
